**I have started my own lab at Yale University!** For up-to-date information on our research, team, publications, and open positions, please visit the new lab website:

<p style="font-size: 1.3em; margin: 0.6em 0 1.4em 0;">&#8594; <a href="https://kexinzhang-lab.github.io/"><strong>kexinzhang-lab.github.io</strong></a></p>

I am an Assistant Professor at [Yale University](https://www.yale.edu/), where my lab develops physics-based and AI-driven methods for cryo-EM, high-throughput visual proteomics, and structure-based drug discovery.
Before joining Yale, I was a postdoctoral fellow with [Nikolaus Grigorieff](https://grigoriefflab.umassmed.edu/) at UMass Chan Medical School, and earlier I completed my PhD with [Aaron Frank](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aaron-frank-9801ab17) at the University of Michigan-Ann Arbor.
You can find more about my research [here](https://kekexinz.github.io/research/).

**Interested in joining?** We are recruiting graduate students, postdocs, and research staff. See the [lab website](https://kexinzhang-lab.github.io/) for details and feel free to reach out.
